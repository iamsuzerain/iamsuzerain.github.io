As Domer has noted on his twitter, prediction markets have consistently underpriced the odds of a Fed rate hike this year by a significant amount compared to CME's /ZQ 30-Day fed futures. I think there could be a number of reasons for this, the two more important ones pull each side into opposite directions and widen the spread.

On the CME trader /ZQ side, there's a lot of hedging going on. Since funds tend to be net long, a sudden rate hike is seen as a risk to the book and therefore there's a bid for hedging above where the fair market price might be. This is the same reason why selling tail insurance tends to be profitable unless you blow yourself up.

On the prediction market trader side, there's definitely a bit of low-trust conspiracy driven thinking that tends to permeate platforms that were built on crypto. The idea that Trump can unduly influence the Fed and get Warsh to do what he wants likely has more purchase with this cohort than with your fund-based CME trader.

I had a lot of money sitting on my PM account, so I was happy to play the arbitrage. I entered Yes positions on the 25 bps hike around 50c and bought /ZQ at a value that suggested around 57% odds of a hike (and therefore shorting the odds of a hike). I sat with my orders and acted as a liquidity provider and market maker on Polymarket, additionally earning that way. Due to negative risk conversions, the final P&L on Poly is a little tricky to reconcile, but net of the loss on the /ZQ side I think the trade ended in the neighborhood of +17k.

The only real downside is how distorted this makes my charts. My standalone IBKR and Poly charts are very distorted, and my risk metrics on IBKR have gone to hell over the past week as the hike odds went from the 50s to 100. But I'm here to make money, not make my charts look pretty. In general, this has been pretty profitable even though I bought a genuinely colossal amount of +50 bps hike even though I knew it was overpriced for fair value, just to cover my tail risk of holding 411 contracts of /ZQ. It's something I likely wouldn't have done in the past, but I've traded for too long and had too many random blow ups to risk another heart attack. If the spread between Poly and /ZQ's implied hike probability is this big going into late October's meeting, I'll do it again.
