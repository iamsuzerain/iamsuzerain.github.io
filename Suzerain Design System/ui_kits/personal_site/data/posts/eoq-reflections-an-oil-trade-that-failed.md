It's the end of a very stressful and exasperating quarter so some reflections here. First of all, the topline numbers aren't terrible, we're still slightly up on the quarter and I've done a lot worse than that. However, in contrast with the monster index recovery, the performance is pretty terrible and the key culprit is I misread how financial and in particular oil markets would react to the Iran War and Strait of Hormuz closure.

The first part of the quarter was annoying but tolerable; I gave back some gains on shorting NKD as a long oil proxy as the AI trade overwhelmed any short term supply concerns in Japan. My short equity delta bias took a decent hit from the AI rally, but ironically it was my long oil positions that kept my account afloat and making money in this period.

Which brings us to this month. I had notionally over 100k of exposure in oil touch options on Polymarket, which have poor liquidity but overprice tails relative to CBOE option markets. I thought this was a relatively +EV way to bet on oil staying elevated. The key point is I thought even with a strait reopening, the supply to demand ratio was distorted that oil would not drop sufficiently to lose me those contracts.

Well it did, and in retrospect I should’ve done better research on how supplied Asian refineries are for the summer, which kept demand constrained even as supply flooded the market in the form of SPR releases and the strait reopening. So now I’m running a sweet -87k lifetime P&L on “finance” contracts on Polymarket, even though the actual purpose I opened the account - to bet on politics, is still very positive and enough to keep Polymarket P&L to a relatively small loss. But of course it’s strange that my Polymarket P&L is down because of financial market positions that I chose rather than the main reason I planned to use the site. The only consolation is that I don’t think I would’ve done better if I had represented those oil longs on IBKR - I was ready to “be long” at 80 and it dropped all the way to 68, so better liquidity to roll the options wouldn’t have helped. Overall, I’m down a bit on the year on all “oil related” trades on IBKR and Polymarket combined, but its not a brutal amount of damage compared to my overall P&L.

Now I have to shift my thinking since my overarching thesis for the past few months has essentially failed. Oil could yet still go up, with how hollowed out inventories are in the medium term, but I think markets could accept low inventory levels if the delta on those inventories flips back to positive. I could continue to play the floor price trade even after this loss, since SPR refills should provide a floor. But maybe it’s time to move on to something else. IV has downshifted a lot, making this less attractive risk/reward wise than it was when it seduced me into taking a large position going into June.
